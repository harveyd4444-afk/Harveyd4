What is this?
This tab shows newly registered UK companies sourced automatically from the Companies House register — the official UK government database where every business must register by law. The workflow runs daily, pulling companies incorporated in the last 150 days in Manchester, verifying they have an active website, and scoring them against Vedit Media's ideal client profile using AI. Only companies scoring 65 or above are saved here.

A newly registered company is a warm prospect — they're building their brand from scratch, have no existing agency relationship, and are making decisions about marketing early. Getting in front of them now means Vedit is the first agency they speak to.
📊 In August 2026 alone, 1,513 companies were incorporated in Manchester — roughly 50 new businesses every single day

What is this?
This tab shows established UK companies actively hiring for social media or content roles on LinkedIn. A company posting for a Social Media Manager or Content Creator is sending a clear signal — they have a marketing budget, they've identified a content gap, and they're ready to spend. That makes them a significantly warmer prospect than a cold outreach.

Rather than applying for the role, Vedit Media can reach out directly and offer to handle their content needs as an agency — often for a similar monthly cost to hiring in-house, but with the full expertise of a specialist team. Each lead includes AI-researched company context and two ready-to-send personalised outreach emails.
🎯 These companies are actively spending on marketing — they have budget and have already identified the problem Vedit solves

1

Apify LinkedIn Jobs Scraper Free tier

Searches LinkedIn for job posts matching keywords like "Social Media Manager", "Content Creator" in the UK. All search parameters are controlled from the Edit Fields node — no node editing required.

2

Filter Agent — binary gate Gemini Flash Lite

Checks each job post against Vedit's ICP and red flag phrases — NHS, SEO roles, PR, B2B, copywriting. Returns proceed: true or false. Temperature 0 for consistent decisions across every run.

3

Duplicate check against the Sheet

Before researching, the workflow queries the LinkedIn Leads Sheet to check if the company already exists. Prevents the same company being processed and emailed twice across separate runs.

4

Research Agent Gemini Flash Lite

Fetches the company website and extracts structured data — products, audience, social presence, size. Low confidence leads are discarded rather than passed to the email agent with thin information.

5

Hunter.io contact finding Free tier

Passes the company domain to Hunter.io's domain search API. Returns publicly known professional email addresses associated with that domain. No contact found → lead discarded silently.

6

Email Writing Agent Gemini Flash Lite

Receives the job post, company research, Hunter contact, and Vedit's ICP. Writes two email variations — one leading with the job post angle, one with a commercial observation. Both saved to Sheets ready to send in one click.
